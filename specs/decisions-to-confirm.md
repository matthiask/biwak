# Decisions to confirm

Decisions made without asking first, for the user to go through. Each is
built and written into its spec; once confirmed (or changed), delete the
entry; git keeps it.

## Quiet hours and away (`quiet-hours.md`, 2026-11-22)

- "As usual when only one is waiting" read as one mail's worth (one
  item, or one batch of files or assigned to-dos), not one notification.
- What waited goes at the start of the working time without the
  10-minute merge; a change a minute before still comes along.
- "Capped like digests": digests have no cap now, so neither has the
  return digest.
- Working hours can't span midnight (until after from); quiet hours on
  need at least one working day. Turning quiet hours off keeps the days
  and hours for next time.
- Away ends at midnight after the date: without quiet hours, the
  return mail goes at 00:00 (digest delivery: 07:00).
- The away sign is a small moon at the mark's lower right; the title is
  "Ada Example" and "Away until 3 Dec: note" on a second line.
- Wording: "Out of office" (settings heading), "I'm back", "Welcome
  back." (after it), "Set away…" and "End away" (admins, on the
  person's page), "Set Ada away" (the modal's title), "In the time zone
  Europe/Zurich, the same for everybody."
- German: Ruhezeiten, Abwesenheit, "Abwesend bis 3. Dez.: …", "Ich bin
  zurück".
- "Teams' entries" read as the members on a team's page and in the
  People page's Teams section; picking a team shows the note of each
  member away.
- The CLI's `members` and `people` show "(away until 2026-12-03)" after
  the name.
