# Clients

A client is a person outside your organization, invited to the
projects they work on with you. Clients see only what staff show them.
Everybody can read this chapter, so you know how it looks from the
other side.

## What clients see

- **Only their projects:** a client sees a project only if they are a
  member, even when it is open to all staff.
- **Only what is shown:** inside it, only items that are visible to
  clients. Everything else doesn't exist for them: pages answer "not found", search, the activity feed,
  the palette, mails and the API leave it out.
- **Only people they work with:** a client sees the members of the
  project and people who act in what they see (a comment's author, for
  example), not the rest of the staff.

## Showing and hiding

Staff decide per container. The switch is on **lists**, **folders**,
**check-ins** and on **messages, docs and files in the root folder**.
What is inside a list or folder follows it and has no switch of its
own. Everything is internal until it is shown: better to show too little
once.

- **Show to clients** asks first and says what changes ("Makes 12
  to-dos and 34 comments visible to 3 clients"). Hiding is one click
  ("Hide from clients") and hides the content again, with what clients
  wrote in it.
- Moving an item into a visible folder or list shows it to clients;
  moving it into an internal one hides it.
- There are no internal comments on visible items: whatever is written
  under them, clients read.
- What happened before something was shown stays hidden from clients
  also afterwards (its earlier titles and events), and so do the older
  versions of docs and files.

## How you can tell

Staff never have to guess:

- a **"Clients" badge** next to everything clients can see, wherever it
  is listed (lists, to-dos, messages, docs, files, search results and
  the to-dos page; the feed and the lantern don't carry it, the item's
  page does);
- a **banner** "Clients can see this." on the page of such an item and
  above its comment and edit forms;
- mails about such items say so in their first line;
- the filter "Visible to clients" on the feed and the to-dos page.

## What clients can do

- Create to-dos in lists they can see, comment, react, and post
  messages. What a client creates is visible to clients.
- Change every field of a visible to-do (title, notes, assignees, due
  date, repeat), complete it and move it between visible lists. They
  can assign members of the project only.
- Answer check-ins they are asked.
- Change what they created. They can replace files they or clients of
  their organization uploaded.

Clients can't create lists, folders, docs or files, pin messages, use
the visibility switch, or see internal items, history and older
versions. They sign in with a mail code or a passkey, or through their
organization's Google or Microsoft account where it's set up
([Signing in](sign-in.md)).
Their notification settings work like everybody's
([Notifications](notifications.md)).

## Partners

Staff of another organization (freelancers, partner agencies) are staff
who see only the projects they are members of, and, like clients, only
the people in them.
